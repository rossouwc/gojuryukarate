## Important naming and teaching distinctions

**Soto/uchi uke:** the package retains the original website's outside-to-inside soto and inside-to-outside uchi convention. Seigokan's chart calls the latter action *shomen uke*. National Goju Karate NSW uses *soto* for outward and *uchi* for inward. These labels are not consistent across all Gōjū-ryū organizations. Match the website's definitions to the dojo's terminology before publishing. [Seigokan technique chart](https://www.seigokan.com/2008/11/japanese-english-technique-table.html), [National Goju Karate NSW](https://gojukaratensw.com/terminology/).

**Yoko geri:** the retained English label “side kick” is illustrated as a lateral heel/edge thrust. Some Gōjū-ryū syllabi reserve *yoko geri* for a circular side action and use *sokuto geri* for the edge kick. This is a chosen interpretation, not a universal naming rule. [Seiwakai moving-basics syllabus](https://www.gojuryu.net/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/Kihon-Ido-Ichi_-Moving-Basics-No-3.pdf).

**Single-frame limits:** soto/uchi, uraken, oi/kizami, and mawashi uke especially need accompanying movement instruction. No arrows or text were embedded in the images, as requested. Numeric weight percentages and force direction cannot be verified from a photograph. Exact thumb positioning in distant hands should be checked at full resolution.
